Supported Payment Methods

Jishora exclusively supports USDT (Tether) deposits and withdrawals to ensure stability and fast transactions.

USDT (TRC20)

Tron network - Fast transactions with minimal fees (≈1 USDT)

USDT (BEP20)

BNB Chain - Fast transactions with low fees (≈1 USDT)

USDT (Aptos)

Aptos network - Fast transactions with low fees (≈1 USDT)

USDT (ERC20)

Ethereum network - Widely supported but higher gas fees

Deposit Information

1

Minimum Deposit: 10 USDT

The minimum amount you can deposit is 10 USDT. There is no maximum limit.

2

Instant Credit

Deposits are credited to your account immediately after blockchain confirmation.

3

Blockchain Confirmations

TRC20 and BEP20 confirm in ~1 min, Aptos in ~1 min, and ERC20 in ~3 mins after network confirmations.

4

No Deposit Fees

Jishora does not charge any fees for deposits. You only pay network fees.

How to Deposit

1

Go to Deposit Page

Click "Deposit" from the home page or navigation menu.

2

Select Network

Choose TRC20 (Tron), BEP20 (BNB Chain), Aptos, or ERC20 (Ethereum) network.

3

Copy Wallet Address

Copy the displayed wallet address or scan the QR code.

4

Send USDT

Send USDT from your wallet to the provided address. Wait for confirmation.
