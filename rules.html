Platform Rules & User Guidelines

General Rules

One Account Per User

Each user is allowed only one account. Multiple accounts will be suspended.

Account Security

Keep your login credentials secure. Never share your password with anyone.

Real Information

Provide accurate personal information during registration and verification.

No Fraudulent Activity

Any attempt to manipulate or cheat the system will result in permanent ban.

Task Completion Rules

1

Complete Tasks Honestly

Follow all task instructions carefully and complete them genuinely. Fake submissions will be detected and rejected.

2

Time Limits

Complete grabbed tasks within the specified time limit. Expired tasks will be automatically cancelled.

3

Daily Task Limits

You can complete up to 10 tasks per day based on your VIP level and account balance.

4

Submission Verification

All task submissions are verified. Provide accurate proof and screenshots when required.

Deposit & Withdrawal Rules

Minimum Deposit: 10 USDT

The minimum deposit amount is 10 USDT (TRC20, BEP20, Aptos, or ERC20).

Minimum Withdrawal: 10 USDT

You can withdraw a minimum of 10 USDT to your wallet.

Processing Time

The withdrawal process is completed within 10–15 minutes. In most cases, it is completed instantly.

Wallet Verification

Ensure your withdrawal wallet address is correct. Transactions cannot be reversed.
